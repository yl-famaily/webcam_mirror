# Store listing — English (en-US)

## Product name
Webcam Mirror

## Short description
Keep a small, always-on-top webcam window on your desktop. Move it anywhere, reshape it, blur or remove the background, and stay in frame — all processed on your own PC.

## Description
Webcam Mirror puts a small live webcam window on top of your desktop. It has no title bar and no borders, so it stays out of the way while you record, stream, present, or simply check how you look.

Drag it anywhere on screen. Pull any edge to resize it in eight directions, or lock it to a ratio — 1:1, 4:3, 3:4, 16:9, 9:16, 3:2, 2:3 — so it keeps its shape no matter which corner you pull. Double-click to switch between a circle and a rectangle. Scroll the wheel to zoom.

BACKGROUND REMOVAL THAT RUNS ON YOUR PC
A built-in person-segmentation model separates you from whatever is behind you. Make the background fully transparent so your wallpaper shows through, blur it, or replace it with a solid color or your own image. The model runs locally on your machine — the app has no network features and sends nothing anywhere.

AUTO FRAMING
Let the window follow you as you move, and set how quickly it follows and how tightly it crops.

RETOUCH
Skin smoothing, skin tone from cool to warm, and brightness. Every slider updates the picture as you drag it.

A SETTINGS PANEL THAT DOES NOT INTERRUPT YOU
Sliders live in a panel that opens beside the window and never takes keyboard focus, so it is safe to open in the middle of a stream or a call.

GLOBAL SHORTCUTS
Ctrl+Alt+M — show or hide
Ctrl+Alt+F — mirror flip
Ctrl+Alt+T — always on top
Ctrl+Alt+C — click-through
Ctrl+Alt+S — settings panel

Click-through lets the mouse pass straight through the window to whatever sits behind it, so the overlay never gets in the way of your work.

Your position, size, shape, camera choice and every other setting are saved and restored the next time you open the app.

Requires Windows 10 version 1809 or later and a webcam.

## Product features
- Frameless, always-on-top webcam window you can drag anywhere on your desktop
- Resize from any of eight edges, or lock it to 1:1, 4:3, 3:4, 16:9, 9:16, 3:2 or 2:3
- Circle or rectangle, switched with a double-click
- Remove, blur or replace the background, processed entirely on your own PC
- Transparent background mode lets your wallpaper show through the window
- Auto framing keeps you centered, with adjustable follow speed and crop size
- Skin smoothing, skin tone and brightness, all adjustable while you watch
- Adjustable opacity, border thickness and border color
- Click-through mode passes the mouse to the window behind
- Global hotkeys for show/hide, mirror, always-on-top, click-through and settings
- System tray icon, and a picker for switching between connected cameras
- Every setting is saved and restored the next time you launch
